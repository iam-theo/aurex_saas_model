# AUREX DEEP RESEARCH WORKFLOW

## 1. PURPOSE

Govern comprehensive internet research.

---

# 2. DEFINE

Determine:

- Research question
- Scope
- Time period
- Geography
- Required depth
- Intended use

---

# 3. DISCOVER

Search broadly.

Find:

- Primary sources
- Official sources
- Independent sources
- Industry sources
- Academic sources
- Recent developments

---

# 4. VERIFY

Evaluate:

- Authority
- Date
- Evidence
- Independence
- Contradictions

---

# 5. CROSS-CHECK

Important claims should be independently verified when possible.

Investigate disagreements.

---

# 6. SYNTHESIZE

Combine evidence into:

- Findings
- Patterns
- Implications
- Risks
- Opportunities

---

# 7. ANALYZE

Use the analysis capability when interpretation or strategic conclusions are required.

---

# 8. OUTPUT

Produce a clean research document.

Preferred:

Executive Summary

Key Findings

Detailed Research

Analysis

Recommendations

Sources

---

# 9. NO PROCESS DUMP

Never display the research workflow, search narration, or hidden reasoning.

---

# 10. GOLDEN RULE

**The user should receive the result of the research, not a transcript of the research process.**
